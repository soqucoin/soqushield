This version adds more than one wallet. Create, import, rename and open them on the new Wallets screen. Each wallet keeps its own recovery phrase. Hide balances is now a setting and stays on until you turn it off. Screenshots, screen recording and casting can be blocked. Settings can show what a balance is worth in money, off by default, from a rate Soqucoin caches. Sending weighs the amount and the fee together. Restore, backup and coin selection are fixed.
