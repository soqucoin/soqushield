SoquShield is the self-custodial wallet for Soqucoin, a Layer 1 network built on post-quantum cryptography. Every transaction is signed on your device with ML-DSA-44 (FIPS 204), the NIST-standardized lattice signature scheme designed to resist attacks from future quantum computers.

YOUR KEYS, YOUR COINS
Your keys are generated and stored only on your device, protected by your passcode and biometrics. A 24-word recovery phrase and an encrypted backup file back up your wallet. Soqucoin Labs never holds your funds and cannot move them.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Create a wallet, or restore one from its 24 words
- Show your address as text or as a QR code
- Send and receive SOQ with post-quantum signatures
- Read your history in Activity
- Field Manual: a full in-app user guide, no account needed

NETWORKS
A new wallet is created on Mainnet, where balances and sending are live. Stagenet, the public test network, is one tap away in Settings for practising with test coins.

BUILT IN THE OPEN
Soqucoin's node software is open source and has been audited by Halborn. The wallet reads chain state through Soqucoin Labs gateways. No sign-in, no ads, no third-party analytics, no data sold.

Questions? The in-app Field Manual and https://soqu.org/help cover setup, sending, receiving, and recovery.
