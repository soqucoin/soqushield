This version adds more than one wallet. Create, import, rename and open them on the new Wallets screen. Each wallet keeps its own recovery phrase. Hide balances is now a setting and stays on until you turn it off. Your wallet is blurred in the app switcher. A setting can show it. Settings can show what a balance is worth in money. It is off by default. The rate is one Soqucoin caches, and the app shows its source and its age. Sending weighs the amount and the fee together before it lets a transaction through. Restore, backup and coin selection are fixed.
